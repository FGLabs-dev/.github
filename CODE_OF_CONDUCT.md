# Community conduct

FGLabs welcomes constructive contributions and respectful discussion from people of all backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expectations

- Treat others with respect and assume good intent when asking for clarification.
- Keep criticism focused on the work; explain disagreements with evidence and concrete suggestions.
- Respect people's privacy. Do not publish personal information, private messages, credentials, or sensitive logs without permission.
- Keep discussions relevant and avoid spam, harassment, discriminatory language, threats, and unwanted sexual attention.

These expectations apply to FGLabs issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-managed community spaces.

## Reporting and moderation

Report concerns privately to [inquiries@fglabs.dev](mailto:inquiries@fglabs.dev). Include links or examples where available, sharing only what is necessary to explain the concern.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close disruptive conversations, or restrict participation. Responses should reflect the severity and repetition of the behavior. Reports will be handled with discretion; absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.
